Phibe Orb Lab

Twenty-four directions for the new Orb, in four rounds. Rounds 3 and 4 keep today's Phibe: the dark round face, the same eyes and a cloud of mist. Round 4 has no ring around the face and breaks apart like the menu bar: into pixels and Fibonacci numbers, or into pixels only. Every one is only a face on a body that is made of pieces, so it can fall apart into numbers, dots, drops or shards and gather again. No arms, no hands, no limbs. Click a card to dissolve or rebuild it; they also do it on their own.

Brief followed: more business than mascot (navy, mint, cyan; no glow-cloud look); tied to the brand (the golden ratio, Fibonacci 3-5-8-11-16, the dot that moves from the b to the e); a body that can really disintegrate and come back; only the little face stays whole.
