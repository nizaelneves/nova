# Name and logo plan (Phibe / Phibi) and the big logo brainstorm

Prepared 2026-10-06. Nothing is renamed in the code yet. This file is the hand-off for the next session.

## 1. Phibe or Phibi: which is easier to say?

| | Brazilian Portuguese reader | American English reader |
|---|---|---|
| **Phibi** | "Fi-bi" (Ph = F, final i = i). One reading. | "FAI-bee" (they know the letter phi as "fye"). One reading, two clear syllables. |
| **Phibe** | Final unstressed "e" is said "i" in Brazil, so it also comes out "Fi-bi". Same as Phibi. | Ambiguous: "FAI-b" (like tribe), "FEE-bee" (like Phoebe) or "FAI-bee". Three readings. |

**Verdict: Phibi.** For Brazilians it is the same either way, and for Americans Phibi removes the ambiguity. It also looks friendlier (like a nickname: Bibi, Fifi) and it keeps the Ph = φ link. What Phibe has and Phibi loses is the "be" (becoming) pun; it can live in the tagline instead ("Phi, be.").

Also check **Phiby** (Y ending) as a fallback if Phibi is taken. Verdict on Phibo: no (rhymes with "bobo" in Portuguese, closes the audience to men only).

Say it like this in the brand: **Phibi (FI-bi in Portuguese, FAI-bee in English).** Two accepted ways to say it is fine; the spelling is what must be unmistakable.

## 2. Registry and availability checklist

- [ ] **USPTO trademark search** (classes 9, 42, 35).
- [ ] **EUIPO eSearch plus** (classes 9, 42, 35).
- [ ] Note every hit with the same or a close sound in classes 9, 42 or 35. A "Phibe" for shoes is not a problem; a "Fibi" for software is.

### 2.4 Meaning and search checks
- [ ] Google "phibi", "phibe", "phiby" (first two pages): who owns the search results?

**Exit criteria for the name:** no identical or confusingly similar mark in classes 9/42/35 in Brazil, the US and the EU; the `.com.br` and one of `.ai`/`.app` free; main handles free or nearly free; pronunciation test passed.

## 3. The audience and what it means for the brand

Brazilian marketers, solo entrepreneurs, traffic managers, copywriters, designers. They are busy, visual, allergic to corporate AI language and to "revolutionary" promises. They value speed, craft and a tool that feels like a smart colleague, not a robot. They post screenshots: the logo and the Orb must look good in a screenshot and in a reel. Portuguese first, English second.

## 4. The big logo brainstorm

Rules of the game: it must read at 16 px (tray, favicon), work in one colour, work on dark (the app is dark) and on light (a landing page), and be ownable (not another sparkle, brain, circuit or robot face). The mascot of the app is the **mist Orb** (dark round face, two glowing eyes, soft mist), so the logo should talk to it.

### A. Wordmark ideas
1. **Eyes as the dots of the i's.** "phibi" in lowercase: both i's have glowing mint dots, which are the Orb's two eyes. The name itself becomes the face. (My favourite: it costs nothing, it is instantly the character, it is unique.)
2. **φ ligature.** The letters "Ph" replaced by the Greek φ: "φibi". Reads "phibi" to the curious, looks like a symbol to everyone. Risk: may be read "fibi" without the h. Fine, same sound.
3. **Golden-rectangle wordmark.** Letter widths and spacing built on 1 : 1.618. Invisible to most, a story for designers.
4. **b with a spiral counter.** The bowl of the b is a golden spiral.
5. **Disintegrating tail.** The last i dissolves into small Fibonacci numbers (1 1 2 3 5) drifting off, the brand's "constant evolution".
6. **Mist behind the dots.** Plain wordmark, a faint moving mist halo behind the two dots only (animated version of idea 1).
7. **Stacked "phi / bi".** φ-i on top, bi under it, like a small poster; good for the app icon of a wide splash.
8. **Soft handwritten wordmark.** A friendly script for the audience that likes personality. Risk: less serious.
9. **Chunky rounded wordmark.** Fat geometric letters, very legible at small sizes, friendly for solo entrepreneurs.

### B. Symbol ideas (a mark that can live alone)
10. **The Orb mark:** a dark circle, two glowing eyes, a thin mint ring. Flat version of the mascot. The simplest and the most consistent with the app.
11. **φ as a face:** the φ circle contains the two eyes; the vertical stroke becomes a little body or antenna.
12. **Eye spiral:** an eye whose iris is a golden spiral. Says "I look at your work" + Fibonacci.
13. **Fibonacci squares as a speech bubble:** 1-1-2-3-5 squares forming a chat bubble with the spiral inside. Says "assistant" and "Fibonacci" in one shape.
14. **Honeycomb growth (the bee nod):** hexagons in groups of 1, 1, 2, 3, 5 growing like a hive, one cell glowing. Bee + Fibonacci, works as a pattern for the whole brand.
15. **Two wings:** two overlapping ellipses that are also two eyes and the infinity-like shape of a bee's wings. Abstract, elegant.
16. **A mist stroke that draws a φ (or a b):** one smoke-like calligraphic line. Beautiful, harder to make crisp at 16 px.
17. **A circle that is half solid, half dissolving into dots:** "the thing that is becoming". Strong story, needs care to stay legible small.
18. **The golden spiral inside a circle, with one dot:** the dot is the eye. Quiet, geometric, premium.

### C. Ideas aimed at the marketing audience
19. **From rough to final:** a rough scribble circle that resolves into a clean one (draft to final copy). Good as an animation (loading, splash).
20. **A cursor arrow with two eyes** for the design crowd (Figma people). Fun, risk of looking like a cursor ad.
21. **A pen nib whose slit is the φ line** for copywriters. Beautiful, but narrows the product to writers.
22. **A target or funnel whose rings follow the golden spiral** for traffic managers. Subtle: the spiral is the funnel.
23. **A tiny "ok" check inside the eye** (done, approved). Too busy; maybe only as a badge.

### D. Lockups and sizes
- **Horizontal:** symbol + wordmark; **stacked:** symbol over wordmark; **symbol only:** app icon, tray, favicon.
- **App icon:** the Orb on a deep navy rounded square.
- **Tray icon (16 to 24 px):** just the two eyes in a circle, one colour.
- **Favicon:** the two eyes.
- **Sticker/social avatar:** the Orb with the mist halo.
- **Loading animation:** idea 19 or the mist condensing into the symbol.

### E. Colour and type directions
- **Core (already in the app):** cyan-to-green (#10c3d3 to #20e7aa) on deep navy (#070b10, ink #14123f).
- **Warm accent for contrast on light:** peach (#ffe3bd to #f6c38f), used sparingly (the snail's body colour).
- **Light version:** the same gradient on white with navy text; check contrast (the mint is weak on white, so use the darker teal #0f9488 for text).
- **Fonts to try (free):** Sora, Outfit, Manrope, Lexend, DM Sans, Space Grotesk (geometric and friendly), Fredoka (soft). Poppins is familiar to Brazilians, but very common.
- **Avoid:** sparkles (✦), brains, circuit boards, neon gradient overload, 3D blobs, robot faces, chat-bubble-with-a-smile clichés.

### F. Voice and taglines (pt-BR first)
Tone: direct, calm, light humour, no jargon, never "revolucionário".
1. "Seu assistente em constante evolução."
2. "Sem pressa. Com resultado."
3. "A pressa é inimiga da perfeição. O Phibi sabe disso."
4. "Menos aba aberta, mais ideia."
5. "O braço direito que não cansa."
6. "Pensa com você. Faz com você."
7. "Phi, be." (the pun, English)
8. "Cresce com o seu trabalho." (the shell that grows)
9. "Seu marketing, em ordem."
10. "Calmo por fora, rápido por dentro."

### G. My ranking for the first round of SVGs
1. **Idea 1 (eyes as the i dots) + idea 10 (the Orb mark)** as a combined lockup. Fastest path to a distinctive, consistent brand.
2. **Idea 2 (φ ligature)** as an alternative wordmark.
3. **Idea 14 (honeycomb growth)** as the pattern and the secondary symbol.
4. **Idea 12 (eye spiral)** as the "premium" symbol option.
5. **Idea 13 (Fibonacci bubble)** as the "assistant" symbol option.

Each route gets: the mark large, at 64/32/16 px, one-colour, reversed (on light), and next to the Orb. Same format as the avatar sketches: SVG first, approval before anything goes into the app.
