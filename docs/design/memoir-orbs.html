1 · SingularityA black sphere with a tilted ring of light. The eyes float inside.

2 · GalaxyA bright core with spiral arms that turn slowly, in two directions.

3 · Nebula SphereA glass planet with a living nebula inside and a small star in orbit.

4 · EclipseA black disc with a bright rim and rays of light. Eyes glow cyan.

5 · PulsarA burning core that sends a beam of light through the sky and pulses rings.

6 · ConstellationA glass sphere holding a turning constellation, with the eyes as two stars.

7 · WormholeA tunnel of light pulling inward. The eyes watch from the far end.

8 · Aurora + CometRibbons of aurora around the orb and a comet circling it.
